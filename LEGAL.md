  works, and a Jackett or Prowlarr server only if you run one and point ztorrent to
  it. Any other search source is one that **you** add, by typing in its address.
  The project does not recommend, endorse or maintain any such source.
- Search results come from the sources you have turned on. The project does not

## Your responsibility

You alone are responsible for how you use ztorrent: for the sources you add, the
torrents you open, and the files you download and share. That includes following
